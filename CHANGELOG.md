# Changelog

Releases of [easyappoint.online](https://easyappoint.online) since the launch. Each date is the day the release went live.

## 2026-11-14, second release

- Organizations pay for analytics 1 490 ₽ a month or 14 900 ₽ a year.
- A new invite link for specialists replaces the organization's previous link, so one link works at a time.
- The section that decides who sees the page comes first: "Кто видит страницу" in an organization's settings, and the catalog switch in a specialist's profile.

## 2026-11-14

- The bots, the site, the user guide and the legal texts say "специалист" instead of "мастер". Old links, buttons and addresses keep working.
- New specialists get bookings at once by default: a client who picks a free time is booked without waiting for confirmation.
- Setup in the bot is shorter and ends with one card that starts the work.
- Organization admins choose the organization's own tags for the catalog.
- New editions of the legal texts. Consents to the earlier editions still count.
- Small fixes in the cabinet's header and menus.
- The site was down for about a minute during this release.

## 2026-10-15

- The complaint button sits at the end of a specialist's or organization's page.
- Corrected disclosures in the privacy policy, and the legal pages no longer carry draft notices.

## 2026-10-14

- Setup in the bot asks how clients book.
- The help groups its topics into first-day steps and short guide cards, with recorded clips of the bot.
- Booking requests are not repeated to the specialist during quiet hours at night.
- One complaint button per specialist or organization page.
- The Telegram menu button shows only an emoji.
- A swipe on the bottom sheet on a phone stays inside the sheet.
- Free time is closed only in the cabinet, not in the bot.
- The pictures in the user guide are drawn in Russian.

## 2026-10-08, launch

The first public release:

- Bots in Max and Telegram, with buttons under messages and built-in guides.
- One account with a client cabinet and a specialist cabinet, a switch between them, and registration with a verified phone.
- Booking on the specialist's page and in the bot, working hours and booking windows, reminders and the evening summary.
- The public catalog with profiles, photos, cities, tags and reviews.
- Organizations of specialists with their own cabinet and calendar.
- Analytics as the only paid feature.
- Checks of texts and photos, and a complaint form on public pages.
- The user guide at [easyappoint.online/help](https://easyappoint.online/help/), with short pages, clips and sketches.
